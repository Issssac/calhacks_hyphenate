# Reconcile: report to the controller

A short report:
- **Ties / doesn't tie**: the overall result, plus balances for AR, AP, revenue and bank.
- **Each finding**: cause, class, evidence, and who it was sent to. Put external entries first;
  those need a person.
- **Feedback raised**: which workflows will revise themselves, and why.

Then stop and wait for the controller to accept the findings or send them back.
