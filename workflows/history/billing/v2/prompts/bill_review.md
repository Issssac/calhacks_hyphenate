# Billing: ask about the variance

Tell the controller, briefly:
- vendor, their invoice reference, the PO;
- each issue from the match, with numbers. For example: "Shackle 1/2in: PO $3.10, billed $3.40 (+9.7%,
  tolerance 2%), 200 units = $60 over." Or: "billed 200, received 150."
- what you recommend: accept, ask for a credit note, or hold until the goods arrive, and why.
- what approving does: the bill is posted as billed, and becomes payable.

Then stop and wait.
