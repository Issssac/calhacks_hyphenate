# Cash: ask the controller

Tell the controller, briefly:
- the money: amount, date, originator, remittance text, and the bank transaction id;
- what you think it pays, with the candidate invoices and their open amounts;
- what doesn't fit (over by $X, unknown payer, a dispute reason) and your recommendation:
  apply it as shown, hold it as unapplied, or chase the customer;
- what approving does: your recommended application is registered in Odoo.

Then stop and wait.
