# Intake: ask the controller

Write a short message for the controller about this quote:

- **Customer and deal**, in one line.
- **The disagreement**: each source's value, with its citation. For example:
  "Email body: 200 @ $4.00 (gmail:…#body). Attached PDF: 200 @ $4.10 (gmail:…#att1:p1)."
- **Your recommendation** and why. For example: "Use $4.10: the PDF is the formal quotation and
  the body looks like a rounded summary." Or: "Ask the customer: the difference is $20."
- **What happens if they approve**: the orders desk drafts a sales order at the recommended values.

Then stop and wait. If they reject, their note is what's right.
