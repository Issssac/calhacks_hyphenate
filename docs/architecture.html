Revenue desk: how the harness and program work

WidgeX harnesses (state machines that drive Claude agents) do the work. Every agent writes to one append-only ledger. Python code, not the agent's word, decides when a workflow may move on and when anything reaches the books or the bank.

Reading it: the top row is the normal path for one order. Rejection drops into the tail (overruled, revise, adopt, promote), and promotion puts the driver back at begin running the new version. The money gate (db.check_post) applies independently of these edges, so a revised machine still cannot post without an approval.

Not drawn, all hard edges out of begin: to revise when there is open feedback for this workflow; to post when the focused order is already approved; to controller_review when one of its requests is still waiting on a decision (ledger mode, e.g. after a restart).
