
====================================================================================================
  WHAT IT IS
====================================================================================================

  An autonomous revenue desk for Halyard Marine Supply (a simulated company). Five agents take a
  customer quote to cash in real Odoo books and a real sandbox bank: intake -> orders -> billing
  -> cash, with reconcile checking all three. Each agent works inside a WidgeX workflow that
  code-checks every step. A controller keeps only the decisions. Every action is appended to one
  tamper-evident ledger.
